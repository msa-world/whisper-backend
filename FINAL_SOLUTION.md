## 🎤 HOW TO USE

### Step 1: Open URL
- Go to: http://localhost:7860/whisper_final.html
- Beautiful dark UI loads

### Step 2: Allow Microphone
- Browser asks for permission
- Click "Allow"
- Status shows "Ready to listen"

### Step 3: Listen to Greeting
- AI automatically speaks: "Hi! I'm Whisper, your AI assistant..."
- Message appears in chat

### Step 4: Speak or Type
**Option A - Voice:**
- Click "🎤 Speak to Whisper" button
- Red "Listening..." status appears
- Speak naturally and clearly
- Live transcription shows what you're saying
- Click button again or stop speaking when done

**Option B - Type:**
- Type your question in the text field
- Click "Send" button
- Or press Enter

### Step 5: Get Response
- AI response appears in chat
- AI speaks the response automatically
- You see text + hear voice
- Continue asking questions
